Rural Japan: Off the Map

Published October 2025 · 8 min read

The Journey

Most visitors to Japan follow a well-worn path: Tokyo, Kyoto, Osaka. We went north into Yamagata, where rice paddies meet cedar forests and mornings smell like woodsmoke.

The shinkansen deposits you in Yamagata City in under two hours from Tokyo. From there, we rented a car — the only practical way to reach the villages we had in mind. Roads narrow to a single lane. GPS loses the plot. That is when the interesting photographs appear.

What the Photographs Show

There is a quality of light in October that photographers spend careers chasing. Low angle, golden, and brief. In Yamagata it lasts about forty minutes. We planned our driving schedule entirely around it.
