County Clare, Ireland · Spring 2025 · 34 photographs

About the Series

The original plan was three days. Three days became three weeks. What started as documentation of a harbour became something else — a portrait of a way of life the rest of the world had stopped noticing.

Mornings on the harbour start before light. By the time the town wakes up, the boats have already left and returned. Most of what they do happens before anyone else is awake to see it.
